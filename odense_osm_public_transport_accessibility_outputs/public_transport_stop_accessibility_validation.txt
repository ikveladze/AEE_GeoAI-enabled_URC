Method notes:
- Access network: OSM walk network, undirected, metric CRS EPSG:25832
- PT data: OSM highway=bus_stop, public_transport=platform/stop_position/station, railway=station/halt/tram_stop, amenity=bus_station
- Access: multi-source Dijkstra from stop/station nodes
- Thresholds: 10 and 20 min walking access to stops, using 1.34 m/s
- Decay: exp(-d/1000)
- Index: 50% nearest all-stop proximity + 50% weighted PT-category composite, min-max scaled to 0–100
- Classes: cutoff class = raw 0; others = quintiles among accessible cells
- Limitation: this is stop accessibility, not schedule/frequency/transfer-based public transport accessibility
