Figure Sx. Redundancy screening of candidate Accessibility–Environment–density features prior to Gaussian mixture modelling (GMM). (a) Pearson correlation matrix for candidate features. Cells with |r| > 0.90 are shown in bold; red outlines mark the discarded pair environmental burden vs environmental quality (|r| = 0.961). (b) Variance inflation factors (VIF) for the retained GMM feature set after correlation screening; the dashed line indicates the VIF = 10 threshold. All retained features have VIF < 10. Environmental burden (†) was excluded from clustering; nested short-threshold accessibility indicators and demographic vulnerability composites were excluded a priori and are not shown.
