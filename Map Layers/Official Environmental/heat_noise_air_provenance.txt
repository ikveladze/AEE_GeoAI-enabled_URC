Heat / noise / air pollution layers for Odense AEE

1) heat_raster.tif
   Source: ESA CLIM4cities Sentinel-3 SLSTR downscaled LST (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.20863040)
   Processing: mean of summer (Jun-Aug) scenes 2020-2023 over Odense bbox; °C; EPSG:25832 ~100 m
   Note: land surface temperature, not air temperature.

2) noise_vector.gpkg / noise_raster.tif
   Source: Vejdirektoratet state-road noise mapping 2022 (Lden bands), ArcGIS REST
   https://kort.vd.dk/.../Stoejkortlaegning_af_statsveje_2022
   Processing: clipped to Odense; midpoint of Isov1-Isov2 dB band; 50 m raster
   Note: state roads only (not full municipal road noise).

3) air_quality_raster.tif (NO2) and air_quality_pm25_raster.tif
   CURRENT FILES: Open-Meteo Air Quality API (CAMS-based) — INTERIM PLACEHOLDER only.
   STRATEGY: Replace with DCE “Luften på din vej” / AirGIS as PRIMARY air indicator.
   Copernicus CAMS / NASA–Sentinel-5P: validation/context only (not main GMM input).
   See: AIR_DATA_STRATEGY_DCE_PRIMARY.md and DCE_AIR_DATA_REQUEST_EMAIL.txt
